Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

At Common Print Co., we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use our branded merchandise storefronts.

Information we collect

When you register for and use a storefront, we collect the following:

  • Account information — your name, email address, phone number, and the organization location you're associated with
  • Order information — your shipping address, the items you ordered, and payment method (Pay Now or Invoice)
  • Payment information — if you pay by card, your payment is processed securely by Square. We never see or store your card number, expiration date, or CVV
  • Usage data — basic information such as when you last logged in, used solely for account management purposes

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Send you order confirmations, shipping notifications, and invoices
  • Respond to questions or concerns you reach out to us about
  • Maintain and improve our storefront platform

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for advertising purposes. We do not share your information with third parties except as necessary to fulfill your order (e.g. shipping carriers) or as required by law.

Payment security

All card payments are processed by Square, a PCI DSS-compliant payment processor. Card data is transmitted directly to Square and never passes through or is stored on our servers. We receive only a payment confirmation token.

Data storage

Your account and order data is stored securely using Google Firebase, which provides industry-standard security and encryption. Data is stored in the United States.

Data retention

We retain your account and order information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period after account closure to fulfill any legal or business obligations. You may request deletion of your account by contacting us at hello@commonprintco.com.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of any non-transactional communications from us

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@commonprintco.com.

Cookies

Our storefronts use session storage (a browser feature similar to cookies) to maintain your cart during a shopping session. This data is cleared when you close your browser tab and is never transmitted to third parties.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our storefronts after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please reach out to us at hello@commonprintco.com. We're happy to help.
